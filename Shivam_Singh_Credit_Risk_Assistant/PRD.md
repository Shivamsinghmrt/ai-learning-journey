# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Project Name
AI Credit Risk & Loan Underwriting Assistant

## Product Vision
Build a decision-support assistant for loan underwriting that helps reviewers assess applicant risk in an explainable and auditable way.

## Problem Statement
Loan decision-making is often a black-box process. This project aims to create a transparent assistant that supports human reviewers with structured reasoning, fairness checks, visible what-if scenario simulation, and applicant-facing explanations.

## Goals
- Provide explainable underwriting support
- Support what-if scenario simulation for income, debt, and loan amount directly in the UI
- Show the impact of each scenario in the assessment output
- Include fairness and audit considerations
- Generate a simple applicant explanation letter

## Non-Goals
- Fully automate final lending decisions
- Replace human underwriting judgment
- Provide real regulatory compliance guarantees
